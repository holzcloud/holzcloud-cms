### Deadline under the CCPA (California and other US states)

A request to know, to correct or to delete is acknowledged within 10 business days of receipt. It is answered within 45 calendar days of receipt. Where necessary, this period can be extended by at most 45 further calendar days; you are told so and given the reason (CCPA regulations § 7021, Cal. Code Regs. tit. 11). A request is verified by comparing the details in it with the details stored, for example with the e-mail address of an order (CCPA regulations § 7060 ff.).
