### Deadline under the FADP (Switzerland)

Information about your personal data is free of charge (Art. 25(6) FADP) and is as a rule provided within 30 days (Art. 25(7) FADP). Where it cannot be provided within 30 days, the controller tells you within that period when you will receive it (Art. 18 of the Data Protection Ordinance, DPO). It takes reasonable measures to identify you as the person the request is about (Art. 16(5) DPO). The English names of the Swiss acts are non-official translations.
