## What remains of an order

If you ask for erasure and have ordered here, the e-mail address and telephone number are erased from your order, and so are the e-mails about it. What is on the invoice the seller must keep: name, company, address, the items ordered, the amounts, the payment state, the return policy and your note on the order. These details remain until the legal retention period in the seller's country ends, counted from the end of the calendar year of the order, and are then erased. Until then they are used for nothing else. The answer to your request names the date.
