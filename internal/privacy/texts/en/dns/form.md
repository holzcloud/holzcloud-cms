## Opting out with the form

You can also opt out here, with or without the signal. Give a name or an e-mail address so that your request can be recorded.

[[widerspruch]]

What you enter in the form is used only to record and answer your request (CCPA regulations § 7025(c)(2)). You need no account, and your request does not depend on proving who you are (CCPA regulations § 7026(c) and (d)). The form sets no cookie and stores neither your IP address nor an identifier of your browser.
