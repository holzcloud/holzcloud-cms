## Nothing is sold or shared

This website does not sell personal information and does not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, as the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) uses "sell" and "share". It loads nothing from other servers and uses no analytics tools.

A business that neither sells nor shares personal information need not offer this page (CCPA regulations § 7010(e), Cal. Code Regs. tit. 11). It is offered anyway: so that you find it when you look for it, and so that your request is recorded if you make one.
