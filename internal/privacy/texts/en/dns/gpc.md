## Global Privacy Control

Global Privacy Control is a signal your browser can send with every request, as the header Sec-GPC: 1. You switch it on in your browser's settings or with an extension. This website treats the signal as your request to opt out of the sale and sharing of your personal information, for this browser (CCPA regulations § 7025). You need to do nothing else.

The first line of the form below shows whether your browser sent the signal (CCPA regulations § 7025(c)(6)). Apart from that the signal changes nothing: it costs nothing, the website looks the same, and no pop-up and no notice stands in your way (§ 7025(f)). The signal is not logged. Only when you send the form is it recorded with your request whether your browser sent it.
