## Orders in the shop

When you order in the shop, the checkout records your name, where given your company, VAT identification number and telephone number, your address, your e-mail address, a note, the items ordered and your choice of delivery and payment. The order is stored in this website's database and shown in the admin area. Without these details the order cannot be carried out. The cookie for your basket is listed in the table above. A confirmation of the order is sent by e-mail through the mailbox the operator has set up for it.
