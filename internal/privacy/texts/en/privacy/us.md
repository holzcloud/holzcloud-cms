## United States: CCPA/CPRA, CalOPPA and other states

This section is for residents of California and of other US states with comparable privacy laws. It follows the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CCPA, Cal. Civ. Code § 1798.100 ff.), its regulations (CCPA Regulations, Cal. Code Regs. tit. 11, § 7000 ff.) and the California Online Privacy Protection Act (CalOPPA, Bus. & Prof. Code § 22575).

Note for the operator, to be deleted before publishing: the CCPA applies to a for-profit business that does business in California and meets one of these thresholds: annual gross revenue of more than USD 26,625,000 (since 1 January 2025); buying, selling or sharing the personal information of 100,000 or more consumers or households; or deriving at least 50 % of its annual revenue from selling or sharing personal information. ### Global Privacy Control

If your browser sends the Global Privacy Control signal, that is the header Sec-GPC: 1, it is treated as a request to opt out of the sale and sharing of your personal information for that browser (§ 7025 of the CCPA Regulations). Nothing on the website changes as a result, because nothing is sold or shared. The page “Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information” shows that the signal was recognised. The signal is not logged; it is recorded only together with a request you send through the form.

### The opt-out form

The form on the page “Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information” stores your name, e-mail address and note as you enter them, the language of the page and whether your browser sent the Global Privacy Control signal, but neither your IP address nor an identifier of your browser. When you send the form, the server keeps your network address (for IPv4 the address, for IPv6 the /64 network) and the block around it (/24 or /48) in memory for at most one hour, to count how many requests come from there and so limit abuse of the form; it is never written to the database or to a file. If a mail server is set up, the operator receives a notification by e-mail. The details are used only to handle your opt-out and are kept {fillin}. Records of requests and of how they were answered are kept for at least 24 months (§ 7101 of the CCPA Regulations).

### Do Not Track

This website does not track you across different websites, and no other party collects information about your activities on other websites through this website. A Do Not Track signal from your browser therefore changes nothing (Bus. & Prof. Code § 22575(b)(5) and (6)).
