### Your rights

You can ask whether personal data about you is being processed and, if so, which (Art. 25 FADP). The information is free of charge and is as a rule provided within 30 days. You can ask for your personal data to be handed over to you or transferred to another controller (Art. 28 FADP), for incorrect personal data to be corrected (Art. 32 para. 1 FADP), and for processing to be prohibited, disclosure to third parties to be prevented or personal data to be deleted (Art. 32 para. 2 FADP). If you have expressly objected to a processing, it may not be continued without a justification (Art. 30 para. 2 let. b FADP).

### Reporting to the supervisory authority

Federal supervisory authority: Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC). The English name is a non-official translation; the authority's official names are in German, French and Italian. You can report a possible breach of the data protection rules to the FDPIC. It opens an investigation on its own initiative or following a report (Art. 49 FADP).
