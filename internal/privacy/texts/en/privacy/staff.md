## Accounts in the admin area

Everyone who works on this website has an account in the admin area. For it, the website stores the name and e-mail address, the password only as an Argon2id hash from which it cannot be worked out, where used the second factor of the sign-in, the current sign-in sessions and a log of the changes someone makes to the website. This concerns only people with an account, not the visitors of the website.
