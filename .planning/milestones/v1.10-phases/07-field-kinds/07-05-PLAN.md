<objective>
Terms as a field kind. `KindRef` is a complete end-to-end template for it and
D-09 copies it wholesale: a chooser instead of a free-text box, a stable identity
in storage, a display value resolved at render time, and an exclusion from block
kinds because a block cannot keep the promise the kind makes.

One thing differs, and it is the whole point. A reference stores an id; a Term
stores the term's **slug**. Renaming a term already keeps its address, so the
slug is the stable identity — and an id cannot be typed into a spreadsheet cell,
which matters because Phase 9's CSV importer will meet these values. The page
prints the term's **name**, resolved fresh on every render, so renaming the term
changes what every page shows without any page being touched.

This work is independent of everything in plans 07-01 through 07-04 and of the
button row in 07-06. It sits here in the order only because it shares three files
with its neighbours — `internal/field/field.go`, `internal/field/render.go` and
`field_input.html` — and two agents editing one kind list is not parallelism.

The fourth task is the half a reading of `KindRef` does not hand you. A reference
crosses a bundle as a page address because an id means nothing on the far
machine; a Term's slug looks portable and is not, because renaming a term keeps
its old address. `internal/bundle/format.go` already answers this for a page's
`Terms` list — a label crosses the manifest as its **name** — and it says in the
same breath that changing that would alter the meaning of every bundle already
handed to somebody. So a Term field's value crosses as a name too, and the import
re-derives the slug exactly as the term store does.

This plan therefore carries four tasks rather than the usual two or three. The
alternative is a plan boundary in the middle of one kind, and the fourth task has
nowhere earlier to go: every line of it names a constant that Task 1 mints, so it
cannot sit in 07-02 where the rest of the bundle work lives. At a projected 75k
against the 100k smart-zone budget it fits, and the fourth task is the smallest of
the four — two switch arms, one store method, one gap closed and one test.

Purpose: close FIELD-03.
Output: `KindTerm`, its chooser, its resolution, its one exclusion, and its bundle
round trip.
</objective>
