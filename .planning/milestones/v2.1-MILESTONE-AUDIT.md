## Cross-phase seams

**Phase 14 is the loudest consumer of phase 13's channel, and that ordering
paid.** The roadmap's note — "building the form first means building it twice" —
held: every visitor-facing sentence the form gained went straight into the
catalogue, and the eight themes' form styling went in after the mechanism had
stopped moving.

The seam back to v2.0 is PUB-01: a regression from `df4ff4c`, whose commit
message justified the change with *"die Texte, die ein Betreiber liest"*. The
error was that they were a **visitor's** texts. The two questions —
*which language does the operator read?* and *which language does this page
speak?* — now have two different answers from the same call, chosen by the
request.

## What the audit found that the phases did not plan for

- **Three bugs that were not the feature.** Every one of them was found by
  building the feature and none by a test:
  1. Every answer to a form submission was built out of the page's **slug**, so
     a form on the start page redirected to `/home` → `/` and lost its query.
     Nobody ever saw the thank-you or the reason on a start page.
  2. `pageName` allows no slash, so a page in a second language lost its prefix
     too.
  3. The consent sentence was stored once per website, so a French visitor was
     asked in German.
- **`Website.Locales()` is a trap and had caught two callers.** It is the
  *extra* languages. `AllLocales()` now exists and both callers use it; the
  comment says why it exists rather than what it does.
- **The test harness was lying by omission.** `loadPlugin` in the e2e tests never
  wired `WithSettings`, so `plugin.Site()` failed inside the module and every
  question a plugin asked about the website was answered with nothing. Fixed;
  the consent test could not have been written without it.
