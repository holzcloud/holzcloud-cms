<objective>
Provoke the one gap `backup.sh` and `deploy/DEPLOY.md` admit to and have never been shown to survive: the database snapshot and the media copy are taken one after the other with the site running. Play pages being saved and media being uploaded (and deleted) during the shipped `backup.sh`, restore each backup on a second container with the shipped `restore.sh`, and compare database and media in both directions, often enough that the outcome is not luck, with proof that the writes actually landed between the two copies. Then force the window with a pause in the shipped script, so that the direction of each possible disagreement and its consequence after a restore are recorded rather than argued.

Purpose: `docs/beta.md` says "the window in which a file is added or deleted in between was not provoked" and REQUIREMENTS CON-01 wants either agreement or the exact window with the run that provoked it. Per 25-CONTEXT.md this is a verification phase: no shipped file is edited here, results are raw output that plan 03 pastes into a record, and what the container cannot show is named, never claimed. FUT-02 (an atomic snapshot) becomes a requirement only if a disagreement shows up whose window cannot be named.

Output: `deploy/test/nightly.sh` with the subcommands `prep consist window` and their pure functions; scratch evidence under `deploy/test/out/amd64-t25-*` (gitignored) that plan 03 repeats from fresh containers.
</objective>
