<objective>
Answer the two remaining questions of the phase with measurements: does a backup made by the release before this one (0.1.0, built from its tag) restore with this release, and what does the backup do when the disk is nearly full but still above the floor that `backup.sh` checks (CON-02, CON-03).

Purpose: `docs/beta.md` lists both as not played ("A backup made by an older binary than the one that restores it was not played in the final set"; "A disk that is nearly full but above `MIN_FREE_MB` was not provoked"). The roadmap wants the same pages, the same login and the migration level the record states, and a recorded outcome for the nearly full disk instead of a guess. Per 25-CONTEXT.md no shipped file is edited unless a measurement proves a defect; that decision belongs to plan 03, this plan records and never edits a shipped file.

Output: `deploy/test/nightly.sh` with the subcommands `compat-source compat capfull` and their pure functions; scratch evidence under `deploy/test/out/amd64-t25-*` (gitignored) that plan 03 repeats from fresh containers.
