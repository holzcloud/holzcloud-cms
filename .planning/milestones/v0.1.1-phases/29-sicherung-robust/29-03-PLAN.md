<objective>
Make the evidence for the status-24 behaviour (ROB-01) a run of the real script in the real container, not only of the fake `rsync` of plan 29-01: add four scenarios to `capfull_run` in which the shipped `deploy/backup.sh` meets a status 24, by hand and under the shipped `holzcloud-backup.service`.

Purpose: phase 25 could say of the unit under a status 24 only "the unit was not played with a status 24". A vanishing file cannot be made on demand at the instant `rsync` reads the tree, so the status is delivered by a stand-in for `rsync` in the container. What is then played is what the script and the unit do with a status 24 (the repeat, the recurrence, the exit status, the sandbox allowing the repeat, the removal of the directory); that `rsync` itself ends with 24 when a file vanishes was seen naturally in 8 of 40 runs in phase 25 and is counted again by plan 29-02's counters. The record says this in a deviation.

Output: changes to `deploy/test/nightly.sh` and `deploy/test/README.md`. After this plan every change to a bound file for the phase is done; plan 29-04 makes the evidence commit.

Hard limits: as in plan 29-01. The stand-in is never placed in the repository's `deploy/` directory and is never part of anything that is shipped to a server; it is written into the container by the harness and removed by it.
</objective>
