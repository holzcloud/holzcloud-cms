<objective>
Teach `deploy/test/nightly.sh` to assert, not merely observe, what the changed `deploy/backup.sh` promises: a failed night leaves no directory of its own and does not block the next night (ROB-02), and to count under load how often a night was repeated after status 24, how it ended, and whether any directory without a `MANIFEST` was left behind (ROB-03 instrumentation). Keep every earlier record's check exactly as it was.

Purpose: the evidence set of plan 29-04 is only as good as its verdicts. Today `capfull_run` calls the outcome of a failed night "a measured property of the shipped script" and passes whatever it finds (`check_info`), `consist_run` has no notion of a repeat or of a left directory, the `window` stage prints a reading of `backup.sh` that names a line that no longer exists, and `record_check` insists on a heading with the words "phase 28". After this plan the harness would fail the set if the removal did not work.

Output: changes to `deploy/test/nightly.sh` (bound file; the evidence commit of plan 29-04 follows all changes to it) and a few lines in the Phase 29 section of `deploy/test/README.md`.

Hard limits: as in plan 29-01. `deploy/test/run.sh` is not edited (it is bound by the earlier evidence). No existing KEY line, PASS line or file name that `deploy/BACKUP-CONSISTENCY-RUN.md` or `28-check.sh` relies on is renamed or removed; lines are added.
</objective>
