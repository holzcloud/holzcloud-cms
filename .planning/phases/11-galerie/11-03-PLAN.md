<objective>
The tax every named, reusable, website-owned thing pays in this codebase, paid
once and in full: a store construction, a handler dependency, a router
dependency, nine routes, two route tests, a `layoutPageNames` entry per screen,
a handler, two templates, a navigation item, an icon, and a translation pass.
`11-PATTERNS.md` §E lists the twelve moves and names the exact line of each.

Two of them are the ones that go wrong, and both go wrong silently.

**The first is `internal/web/render.go:46`.** It is one long hand-maintained
slice of page names. A template that renders through `web.RenderAdmin` and is
missing from it comes out as a bare fragment — no navigation, no flash area, no
`hx-headers` CSRF attribute on the body — and **no test in this repository can
see that**. Phase 8's entire closing gate was one such omission. This plan adds
two names to it and the browser pass in plan 11-07 confirms both by eye, because
that is the only instrument there is.

**The second is `cmd/holzcloud/main_test.go:158`.** That table is the
`adminOnly` list, and it is the safety net that was missing when website
deletion shipped reachable by any editor. An album is **content**, the same as a
menu and a page, so it does not belong there — it belongs in the editor-open
list at `:186-190`. Getting this backwards would lock every editor out of a
feature built for editors, and getting it backwards the other way would be the
2026-09-06 defect again. The gate below asserts the `adminOnly` table is still
19 rows, which is the measurement that says the album was filed as content on
purpose rather than forgotten.

The third thing worth stating plainly: **`admin.NewHandler` is not touched.** It
is positional and already takes 18 arguments, and adding a 19th would edit every
call site including the test router. `SetProductStore`, `SetOrderStore`,
`SetOutbox` and `SetActivityStore` are the established answer and
`SetAlbumStore` is the fifth of them.

Purpose: the editor half of GAL-03 and the reachable half of GAL-05.
Output: an album can be created, named, filled with pictures, reordered, renamed
and deleted, by an editor, with scripting switched off.
</objective>
