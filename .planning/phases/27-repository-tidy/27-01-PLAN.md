<objective>
Regenerate `docs/branches.md` against the current `origin/main`, record one decision per branch with its full tip SHA and tip subject, and write the owner's step list for deleting the branches, with the expected output of each step. This plan reads the remote and changes nothing on it.

Purpose: TIDY-01 asks that each remote branch is decided and the decision written down, and TIDY-04 asks that the two local branches are retired. Both end in an outward-facing, irreversible act that needs the owner's explicit go at that moment (binding adjustment of the orchestrator, which overrides D-01, D-02 and D-08 where they conflict). So this plan delivers everything up to the act: the evidence, the record that makes a restore possible, and the copy-pasteable steps. Plans 27-04 and 27-05 hold the acts, behind a human gate.

Output: a regenerated `docs/branches.md` with a Decisions table, `.planning/phases/27-repository-tidy/27-OWNER-STEPS.md`, and TIDY-01 and TIDY-04 at `Pending owner` in `.planning/REQUIREMENTS.md`.

Hard limits for this plan: no `git push` of any kind (not even to a local path), no branch deleted (remote or local), no tag, no change to `fix/bausteine-befunde` (D-04). The only command that moves anything is `git fetch origin --prune`, which updates remote-tracking refs and forgets gone ones.
</objective>
