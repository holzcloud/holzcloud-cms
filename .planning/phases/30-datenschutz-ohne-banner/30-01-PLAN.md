<objective>
Make the request log stop holding a visitor's full address, and make the time it is held for something the server enforces and not only something a text says (DSV-01, DSV-02; decisions D-01, D-02, D-03).

Purpose: the audit of 2026-10-29 found that `web.AccessLog` writes the client address of every request into the journal untruncated and with no retention limit. The phase goal is a site that needs no cookie banner and has an honest privacy text; an untruncated address kept without limit makes that text untrue before it is written.

Output: `web.TruncateIP` and the three constants other plans read; `AccessLog` using it; the journald drop-in `deploy/journald-holzcloud.conf`; tests that drive a real request through the real router and that tie drop-in, constant and deployment page together.

Scope limits, repeated because they bind this plan: truncation applies to the request log only (D-02). `internal/activity`, `internal/web/clientip.go` and the login throttle are not touched. The other places that write an address into a log line (the login rate-limit warning, the two single-sign-on refusals, the forward-auth wrong-secret warning) are admin-side security events and stay as they are; plan 30-04 makes the privacy text say so, and the final report of this phase names them to the owner. No change to `deploy/DEPLOY.md`, `deploy/holzcloud.service`, `deploy/backup.sh`, `deploy/restore.sh`, the backup units, `deploy/test/run.sh` or `deploy/test/nightly.sh`: the evidence of phases 28 and 29 binds them.
</objective>
