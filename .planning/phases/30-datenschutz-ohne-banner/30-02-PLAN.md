<objective>
Fix the one cookie lifetime the audit found too long and the one Secure flag it found wrong, and make "the public site sets no cookie without the visitor doing something" a test that runs through the real router over all eight shipped themes (DSV-03, DSV-04; decisions D-08, D-09).

Purpose: the phase goal says the public side sets no cookie on its own, and what it does set is necessary or triggered by the visitor. Today that is a property of the code that nobody checks: a new template function, a new middleware or a theme asset route could add a `Set-Cookie` to a plain GET and the first sign would be a complaint. The test makes it a property of the build. The two code changes are what the audit of the three existing cookies turned up: the price-view choice lives a year, and its Secure flag is read from the request, which is empty behind the TLS-terminating proxy the deployment documents.

Output: the renamed and exported cookie constants, `AudienceLifetime`, the Secure fix, and `cmd/holzcloud/public_cookies_test.go` with the fixture builders it needs in `cmd/holzcloud/main_test.go`.

Scope: no new cookie, no change to the basket cookie's lifetime (30 days, `shop.CartLifetime`) or to the unlock cookie (12 hours, path-scoped); the privacy text that lists them is plan 30-04.
</objective>
