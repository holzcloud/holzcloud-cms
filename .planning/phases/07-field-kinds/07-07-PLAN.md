<objective>
The per-kind tax, the standing gate, and the browser pass.

Five kinds were added in this phase. Each one costs a second hour after the hour
that built it: a section in the authoring specification, a filled row in
`SampleData`, an empty row in `MinimalData`, and a pass through the translation
tool. Three tests tie the specification, the fixtures and the loader structs
together, so a kind missing from any one of them fails the suite — that is the
tax working, not the tax being unfair.

Then the gate. ROADMAP criterion 6 has two halves and both are mandatory: the
translation tool reports nothing open and nothing orphaned, **and** everything a
person can see has been driven once through the running application in a browser.
Phase 6 closed the first half and left the second half only partly run; this
phase does not repeat that. The browser pass is driven with a browser tool
against a local dev server, not handed back as a checklist.

The browser pass also carries the phase's one open question. D-08 kept a Range
field usable as a controlling field on the premise that a number input matches
`:placeholder-shown`. Nothing in the test suite can confirm that. This is where it
is settled, by watching a dependent field appear and disappear — or by watching
it fail to, and writing the exclusion the roadmap originally asked for.

Purpose: close ROADMAP criterion 6 and the phase.
Output: the contract documents, green catalogues, and an observed application.
</objective>
